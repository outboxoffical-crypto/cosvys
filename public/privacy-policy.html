Cosvys Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 19 November 2026

Application: Cosvys

Website: https://cosvys.com

1. Introduction

Welcome to Cosvys. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share through our application.

Cosvys is designed to help painting contractors and other users manage painting projects, record room measurements, estimate paint and material requirements, and organize project information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it is stored, and the choices available to you.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Cosvys, we may collect the following information:

A. Account Information

  • Name and email address.
  • Login and authentication information.
  • Other account details you provide during registration.

B. Customer and Project Information

  • Customer names and phone numbers.
  • Customer addresses and project locations.
  • Project types, including interior, exterior and waterproofing.
  • Room measurements, dimensions and calculated areas.
  • Paint product selections, pricing and estimation details.
  • Labour and material calculations.

C. Photos and Uploaded Content

If you use the photo upload feature, we may store photographs related to painting projects, rooms, walls and site conditions.

D. Technical Information

We may collect limited technical information required to operate, maintain and improve the application, such as device information, application diagnostics and crash reports, depending on the services enabled.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Save and manage painting projects.
  • Calculate room areas and paint requirements.
  • Generate project estimates and summaries.
  • Store room measurements and project photographs.
  • Manage product pricing and coverage information.
  • Improve application performance and user experience.
  • Maintain application security and troubleshoot technical problems.
  • Provide customer support.

4. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

Cosvys uses third-party technology providers to support its services.

Supabase: Used for backend services, authentication, database storage and file storage, depending on the features enabled in the application.

Google Play Services and Firebase: If enabled, services such as Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Cloud Messaging may process technical information, usage information, crash reports and notification-related data.

These service providers process information according to their respective privacy policies and service terms.

We do not intend to sell users' personal information to third parties.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the application, maintain its infrastructure, provide support or comply with applicable legal requirements.

Users are responsible for ensuring they have permission to enter or upload customer information and photographs into Cosvys.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored through Cosvys against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no internet-based service or electronic storage system can guarantee complete security.

Users should protect their account credentials and avoid sharing passwords with others.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the application and fulfil legitimate operational or legal requirements.

Users may request the deletion of their account and associated personal information by contacting us using the email address provided below.

Some information may need to be retained where required by applicable law or for legitimate security and legal purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

Cosvys is designed for business and professional use and is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will take appropriate steps to address it.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Ask questions about how your information is used.
  • Withdraw consent where applicable.

You can contact us using the details below to exercise these rights.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in application features, technology, legal requirements or business practices.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
